# Metal Universe

## Coverage

| Metal | Core markets | U.S. proxy | Watch list |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | LBMA, COMEX GC | GLD | Real yields, DXY, Fed, central-bank demand, ETF flows, COT, jewellery/investment demand |
| Silver | LBMA, COMEX SI | SLV | Gold beta, solar/electronics demand, ETF flows, COT, COMEX stocks |
| Copper | LME, COMEX HG | CPER | China activity, global manufacturing, LME/COMEX stocks, treatment charges, mine disruptions, tariffs |
| Palladium | NYMEX PA | PALL | Auto production, ICE/EV adoption, platinum substitution, Russian/South African supply, COT |

## Preferred sources

| Topic | Primary source examples |
|---|---|
| Monetary policy and yields | Federal Reserve, US Treasury, FRED |
| Futures positions | CFTC Commitments of Traders |
| Futures and delivery stocks | CME/COMEX and LME |
| Gold demand and ETFs | World Gold Council, LBMA |
| Platinum-group balances | World Platinum Investment Council |
| Copper balances | International Copper Study Group, IEA |
| Mine disruption or trade policy | Relevant government, producer, exchange, or regulator |

Use liquid ETFs for accessibility, but state vehicle-specific risks such as expense ratio, futures roll, tracking difference, and liquidity. CPER is a futures-based copper vehicle; it is not physical copper.
