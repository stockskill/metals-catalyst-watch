# Catalyst Rubric

## Conditional Buy test

Use **Conditional Buy** only when the long-horizon thesis has at least two independent supports, but one or more timing conditions remain unresolved.

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Structural support | Supply/demand, flow, or policy evidence supporting the thesis |
| Timing condition | An observable trigger: yields/dollar, ETF flow, price structure, inventory, or event outcome |
| Risk control | A clear invalidation condition with the data that would show it |
| Balance | State the strongest contrary interpretation |

## Typical confirmation and invalidation examples

| Metal | Confirmation examples | Invalidation examples |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | Falling/stable real yields, softer DXY, sustained ETF inflows | Higher real yields and stronger DXY alongside sustained ETF outflows |
| Silver | Gold support plus improving industrial demand and flow confirmation | Industrial demand deterioration and loss of precious-metals support |
| Copper | Falling exchange stocks, improving activity data, supply disruption | Inventory rebuild plus weakening manufacturing/China demand |
| Palladium | Verified supply loss or stronger-than-expected autocatalyst demand | Accelerating substitution/EV pressure without a supply offset |

These are analytical criteria, not standing buy or sell rules. Do not attach fabricated numerical thresholds; use observable, dated evidence.
